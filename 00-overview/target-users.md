# Target Users & Personas

## Primary
- **Individual MoMo users** vulnerable to social engineering (e.g., older users, first-time digital payers).
- **Small businesses / traders** receiving and sending frequent payments, targeted by fake buyers/suppliers.

## Secondary (API customers)
- Mobile-money operators, banks, fintech apps, merchants, payment aggregators.

## Personas
**Ama, 52, market trader (Accra).** Sends GHS 20–150 to suppliers/family. Gets a call: "Your account will be
blocked unless you verify by sending GHS 500." Low digital confidence, acts under pressure.

**Kwame, 27, freelancer.** Sends GHS 50 to friends. Finds a "job" that requires a GHS 1,500 "registration fee".

**FinPay (B2B).** Wallet app wanting a drop-in scam-prevention call before every payout.
