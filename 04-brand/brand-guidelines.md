# Brand Guidelines (v0.1)

## Voice & tone
- **Calm, clear, caring.** Never accusatory ("You're being scammed!" ✗ → "Let's double-check this payment." ✓).
- Plain language; short sentences; avoid jargon ("authorized push payment fraud" ✗ → "being tricked into sending money" ✓).
- Ghana-friendly English; consider Twi/Ga/Ewe/Hausa localization as roadmap.

## Risk-state UI language
| Level | Color | Icon | Headline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low | safe-green | ✓ | "Looks normal" |
| Medium | caution-amber | ! | "Take a quick look" |
| High | alert-orange | ⚠ | "This looks unusual" |
| Critical | critical-red | ⛔ | "Let's pause and verify" |

## UI principles
Mobile-first · big tap targets (≥ 44 px) · one primary action per screen · always an obvious "Cancel" · reasons shown as short bullet list.

## Asset folders
`assets/logo`, `assets/icons`, `assets/social`, `assets/mockups`, `exports/` (final files only).
